About "Cardfight!! Vanguard"
--------------------------------

1-on-1 battle card game developed by Bushiroad and launched in February 2011.Localized in English, Chinese, Italian, Thai, Korean, etc., it's sold in 61 countries and regions worldwide, continuing to grow in popularity.

It also includes media such as animations and comics, and in 2021, on the project's 10th anniversary, the new series "CARDFIGHT!! VANGUARD overDress" began.
